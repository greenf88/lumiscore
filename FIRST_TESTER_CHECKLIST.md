# LumiScore first-tester checklist

Please test LumiScore on your usual phone or computer. The full check should take about five minutes.

1. Open the homepage and confirm it fits your screen without horizontal scrolling.
2. Open a book from the homepage, then use **Back to books**.
3. Search for a book or author and open one result.
4. Sign in or create an account, rate one book, and confirm the score updates immediately.
5. Return to the homepage, search for that book again, and confirm the updated score appears.
6. Toggle **Want to Read** and confirm it does not open the book.
7. Switch between Ink and Paper themes.
8. Sign out and confirm you can still browse and search.

When reporting a problem, include:

- what you expected and what happened;
- the book title or page;
- phone/computer model and browser;
- a screenshot if possible.
