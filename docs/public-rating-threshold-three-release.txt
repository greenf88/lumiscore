Privacy trade-off: reducing the threshold reduces protection for small groups.
Neither threshold is an anonymity guarantee. Known individual scores and changes
in a published mean can reveal another score; archived published means cannot
be retracted. Do not claim guaranteed anonymity or add synthetic production
ratings to meet the threshold. Three people must independently rate the SAME book.
